# The Pragmatic Backend Interview Blueprint
## A Realistic 1-Hour Daily Study Plan from 30+ Years of Experience

### Philosophy
*"You don't need to know everything. You need to know enough, deeply, and demonstrate you can learn the rest."*

After 30+ years in tech and mentoring hundreds of engineers, here's the truth: Most study plans fail because they're unrealistic. This blueprint assumes you have a life, a job, and exactly 1 hour daily. It's designed for sustainability and actual results.

---

## Phase 0: Foundation Check (Week 1-2)
**Time: 14 days × 1 hour = 14 hours**

### Reality Check Assessment
```
Day 1-3: Baseline Testing
- Take 1 LeetCode easy (20 min)
- Attempt 1 LeetCode medium (40 min)
- Document where you struggled

Day 4-7: System Design Baseline
- Watch 1 system design video daily
- Try to design Twitter in 1 hour (Day 7)
- Note knowledge gaps

Day 8-14: Current Skills Audit
- Code in your strongest language (30 min)
- Review your recent work projects (30 min)
- List transferable experiences
```

**Milestone**: Know exactly where you stand

## Phase 1: DSA Foundation (Weeks 3-14)
**Time: 12 weeks × 7 hours = 84 hours**

### The "One Pattern Per Week" Approach
```
Week 1-2: Arrays & Strings
- Mon/Wed/Fri: 2 problems (easy → medium)
- Tue/Thu: Review solutions, understand patterns
- Weekend: 1 mock interview problem

Week 3-4: Two Pointers & Sliding Window
Week 5-6: Hash Maps & Sets  
Week 7-8: Trees (DFS/BFS)
Week 9-10: Graphs Basics
Week 11-12: Dynamic Programming (Simple)
```

### Daily Structure (1 hour)
```
First 20 min: New problem attempt
Next 20 min: Solution study (understand optimal)
Final 20 min: Re-implement from memory
```

**Critical Rule**: If stuck for 15 min, look at hints. If stuck for 25 min, study solution. Time is precious.

**Tools**:
- NeetCode.io (organized by pattern)
- LeetCode with timer always on
- Anki for pattern flashcards (5 min daily review)

**Milestone**: Solve 100 problems, recognize 6 core patterns instantly

## Hard-Earned Wisdom

### What Actually Matters
1. **Consistency > Intensity**: 365 hours beats 100 hours in two weeks
2. **Understanding > Memorization**: Learn 5 patterns deeply vs 50 superficially
3. **Communication > Perfection**: Explain your thinking clearly
4. **Fundamentals > Tricks**: Basics get you 80% there

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
1. **Tutorial Hell**: Max 30% learning, 70% doing
2. **Perfectionism**: "Good enough" and move forward
3. **Solo Journey**: Find study partners by Week 4
4. **Ignoring Behaviorals**: They matter more than you think
5. **Not Building**: Real projects > endless problems

### The 80/20 Rules
- 20% of patterns solve 80% of problems
- 20% of system components appear in 80% of designs
- 20% of your projects will impress 80% of interviewers
